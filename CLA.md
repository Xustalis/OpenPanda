## 2. Scope: what this Agreement covers

This Agreement applies to every Contribution **except**:

- **Trivial Contributions** — small fixes such as typos, formatting,
  single-line changes, documentation-only edits, or patches of roughly
  15 lines or fewer. Trivial Contributions are accepted directly under
  the project's outbound AGPL license and need no CLA assent.
- **Adapter scripts** — contributions to files under `adapters/`.
  Adapters are standalone programs communicating with the core over a
  stdio protocol; community adapters are accepted under the outbound
  AGPL license and are not included in commercial-license distributions

Whether a Contribution is trivial is determined by the Maintainer in
good faith; borderline cases are resolved by simply asking you.
